05 / RESEARCH

Early Detection of Bulbar Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) Through Speech Analysis and Neural Networks

This research explores the non-invasive early detection of Bulbar ALS through pathological speech analysis, focusing on acoustic biomarkers and spectrogram-based representations of speech. The work investigates deep learning and ensemble classification approaches to identify patterns in speech that can support earlier detection, with the broader goal of making speech a practical, accessible signal for clinical screening. The research was presented at the International Conference on Sustainable Communication Networks and Applications (ICSCN 2025).
